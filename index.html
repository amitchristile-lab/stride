Your data stays yours

Movara is an activity and health tracker for iPhone and Android. This page explains, in plain language, what Movara does with your information and the terms for using the app and its subscription.

The short version

  • Everything you record is stored on your phone and, if you allow it, in Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Movara has no servers and no accounts. We never receive, sell or share your data.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • Movara is not a medical device. Always follow your doctor's advice.

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

This policy applies to the Movara app on iOS and Android, provided by Amit Kumar ("we", "us"). By using Movara you agree to the practices described here.

Information Movara uses

Movara only uses information on your device to provide its features. None of it is sent to us.

  • Activities you record: type, date, duration, distance, pace, calories, route and notes.
  • Location: used only while you are recording an activity, to measure distance and draw your route. Movara does not track your location at any other time.
  • Motion and fitness data: steps and floors climbed from your phone's sensors.
  • Health data you enter: blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, conditions, medicines and symptom check-ins.
  • Settings: your first name, goals, body weight and preferences, used to personalise messages and estimate calories.
  • Emergency contacts: names and phone numbers you add for the SOS feature.
  • Photos: only a photo you choose yourself for a share picture. Movara cannot see the rest of your library.

Apple Health and Health Connect

With your permission, Movara reads and writes data in Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), such as workouts, routes, steps, distance, active energy, heart rate, blood pressure and blood sugar. You choose exactly which data types to allow and can change this at any time in the Health or Health Connect app.

Health data obtained through Apple HealthKit or Health Connect is used only to provide Movara's features to you. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties and never used for any purpose other than the app's health and fitness features.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on your device, protected by your phone's own encryption and passcode. If you use Apple Health or Health Connect, data you allow is also stored there under Apple's or Google's privacy policies. Your phone's own backups (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include Movara's data if you have backups turned on.

Sharing

Movara does not share your data. Information only leaves your phone when you choose to send it, for example when you share an activity picture or route file, or send an SOS message to your emergency contacts. Those messages go through apps you choose, such as Messages or WhatsApp.

Payments

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple or Google only tell the app whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. By default, medicine reminders do not show medicine names on the lock screen.

Children

Movara is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn off location, motion, notifications or health access at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Delete individual activities and readings inside the app.
  • Deleting the Movara app removes all data Movara stored on your phone. Data you allowed Movara to write to Apple Health or Health Connect can be deleted there.

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.

Security

Movara stores data inside the app's protected space on your device, verifies subscriptions with Apple's and Google's signed records, and offers an optional Face ID or device lock for the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
